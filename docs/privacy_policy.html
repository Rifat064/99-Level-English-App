Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

ShobdoDaily ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by ShobdoDaily.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google, we collect your name and email address to create and manage your profile.
  • App Activity: We store your vocabulary progress, quiz scores, and bookmarks to provide spaced repetition and sync your progress across devices.
  • Crash and Performance Data: We collect anonymous crash logs and performance analytics to identify bugs and improve the app.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide, maintain, and improve the ShobdoDaily experience, including syncing your progress and securing your account.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers (like Supabase for backend infrastructure and Sentry for crash reporting) strictly for operating the app.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app by going to Settings > Delete Account. This action is irreversible.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@shobdodaily.com.
