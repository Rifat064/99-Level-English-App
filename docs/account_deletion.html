Account Deletion Request

ShobdoDaily allows you to easily delete your account and all associated data.

How to delete your account inside the app (Recommended)

  1. Open the ShobdoDaily app on your device.
  2. Navigate to Settings from the top navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your choice.
Warning: Deleting your account will instantly permanently erase your vocabulary progress, bookmarks, quiz scores, and profile information from our servers. Active subscriptions via Google Play must be canceled separately in your Play Store app.

How to request deletion on the web

If you no longer have access to the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us from the email address associated with your ShobdoDaily account.

Email: support@shobdodaily.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request

We will process your request and permanently delete your data within 7 days.
